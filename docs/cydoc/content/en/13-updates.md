## Update process {#process}

Use **Check for updates** at the top of Settings.

```steps
{"items":[{"title":"Check","body":"Compare the installed version with the latest available release."},{"title":"Match the edition","body":"Select installer, full portable or CyAnnota-free portable."},{"title":"Download","body":"Save the matching asset locally."},{"title":"Verify","body":"Validate its SHA-256 digest before offering the file."},{"title":"Install","body":"Close CyCapture and launch the downloaded file."}]}
```

## Available editions {#editions}

- **Windows installer:** standard installation with CyAnnota and its plugin.
- **Full portable:** single-executable CyCapture edition with bundled CyAnnota.
- **Portable without CyAnnota:** lightweight edition without CyAnnota or its plugin entry.
- **Standalone CyAnnota plugin:** DLL and ZIP for manual plugin installation.

The documentation is embedded in every CyCapture edition and extracted locally when opened.
