## Browse captures {#browse}

Open Gallery from the tray. Filter the list to **Today**, **Yesterday** or **All**, then click a tile to open the large viewer. Use Previous/Next or the Left and Right arrow keys.

## Playback {#playback}

Images open in a large preview. Video, GIF and audio use their built-in playback controls when the format is supported. Escape closes the viewer before closing the Gallery window.

## Clipboard {#clipboard}

The Copy action places an image bitmap or a media file reference in the Windows clipboard. File-reference support depends on the destination application.
